Exercise 2: Broadcast replaced with Scatter (sum_scatter.c)
Run on: Debian Linux container (Docker), Open MPI 5.0.7, gcc 14.2, 8 cpus.

Changes from Exercise 1:
- Only root (rank 0) allocates and fills the full 1,000,000-element array.
- Every process allocates only local_chunk with N / size elements (250,000 with 4 processes).
- MPI_Bcast is replaced by MPI_Scatter(array, chunk_size, MPI_INT, local_chunk, chunk_size,
  MPI_INT, 0, MPI_COMM_WORLD). sendcount is chunk_size (elements per process), not N.
- The local loop now goes over local_chunk[0 .. chunk_size).
- The Send/Recv collection is unchanged.
