Exercise 5: Reduce replaced with Allreduce (sum_allreduce.c)
Run on: Debian Linux container (Docker), Open MPI 5.0.7, gcc 14.2, 8 cpus.

Changes from Exercise 4:
- MPI_Allreduce(&local_sum, &total_sum, 1, MPI_LONG_LONG, MPI_SUM, MPI_COMM_WORLD)
  (no root parameter) gives the total to EVERY process.
- Every process prints its local_sum, the total and its share of the total in percent.
- Root still prints the check.

Output (4 processes):

The shares grow with the rank (6.25%, 18.75%, 31.25%, 43.75%) because the later chunks
hold bigger numbers. They add up to 100%.
